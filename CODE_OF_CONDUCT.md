# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Welcome questions and contributors with different levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to protect contributors. Apply moderation consistently, explain decisions when safe, and handle reports confidentially. A private moderation contact must be established alongside the public upstream repository before community launch.
